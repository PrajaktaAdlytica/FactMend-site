# Asset sources and provenance

Created 9 October 2026 for Factmend's selected Sunlit Archive direction.

## Original assets

The Mended Folio mark, icon paths, patterns and explanatory SVG plates were authored for this kit. Logo wordmark letterforms are outlined from Instrument Serif, used under the included SIL Open Font License. Text in diagram plates remains editable. These are concept illustrations, not screenshots of a working product.

Company avatar, LinkedIn banner and Open Graph card are also included under `social/`. These are Factmend compositions, distinct from the official third-party social icons.

## Fonts

Self-hosted official Google Fonts repository files:

- [Instrument Serif](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif): Regular and Italic, `fonts/instrumentserif-OFL.txt`.
- [Inter](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter): variable `Inter[opsz,wght].ttf`, `fonts/inter-OFL.txt`.

No remote font requests are made by the specimen. Keep licence files when redistributing.

## Official social assets

- LinkedIn `[in]` original black, white and colour PNGs from the [official download page](https://brand.linkedin.com/downloads); zip source: `https://content.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/xbu/linkedin-revised-brand-guidelines/logos/in-logo.zip`. [Usage guidance](https://brand.linkedin.com/in-logo). Originals retained in `social/linkedin/in-logo/`.
- Crunchbase blue vector wordmark and original site favicon observed on the [official press page](https://about.crunchbase.com/press). Vector source: `https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/687e6d0359c517be389ea464/699cd6b24bf1f2f372ff4740_cb_logo_bluevector%20(1).svg`. Icon source: `https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/687e6d0359c517be389ea464/6909b8953a896d2ea59effbe_favicon.png`.

Original marks are preserved rather than redrawn. Their ownership remains with their respective companies. The specimen has no active social profile links because Factmend destinations are unverified. No endorsement is implied.

## Reference basis

The user-selected Option 1 Sunlit Archive study supplies the aesthetic direction. The user-provided Startup Website Master Guide supplies the branding-first, two-font, distinctive-section, responsive and complete-product-page requirements. Zep, supermemory, Nitex, Daylight and Hear screenshots are inspiration for section storytelling and visual rhythm; their logos, images and page designs have not been copied into the Factmend kit.
